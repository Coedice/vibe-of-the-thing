Vibe of the Thing

There is no one section... It's just the vibe of the thing. (The Castle, 1997)

A collection of interactive tools for exploring Australian government data.

📊

Budget Daisy

Interactive sunburst visualisations of federal budgets and revenues. Explore spending patterns across government departments from 1964 onwards.
📋

Parliament Kanban

A Kanban view of all federal parliament bills. Track legislation through Pending, Passed, or Failed, and filter by party and status.
⏱️

When Talk

Estimate speaking times for parliamentary proceedings. Enter a bill and expected speakers to estimate when each person will talk.
